Stichting CODINE

Stichting CODINE aims to foster and support relief in the DR Congo. In addition The organization offers a platform that facilitate activate participation of young people in the democratic process both at the local and national level. In addition, the organization fosters intercultural dialogue between young people with migrant background and their host society and youth entrepreneurship, diversity and sustainability.
