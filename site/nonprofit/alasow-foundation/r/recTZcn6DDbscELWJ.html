Alasow Foundation

Stichting Alasow is a Dutch non-profit foundation dedicated to humanitarian relief, education, healthcare, poverty reduction, and sustainable community development. We support vulnerable communities by providing emergency assistance, educational opportunities, clean water projects, livelihood initiatives, and social welfare programs. Our mission is to improve lives through transparency, accountability, and responsible stewardship of donor funds while working in partnership with local and international
