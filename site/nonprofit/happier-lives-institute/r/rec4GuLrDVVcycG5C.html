Happier Lives Institute

The Happier Lives Institute (HLI) is the only organisation that researches the most cost-effective ways globally to improve wellbeing. We connect people and organisations together to fund happiness and improve wellbeing around the world. We fuel the generosity of our donors, guided by happiness science, to make the world a happier place. Our dedication and passion is directed towards finding the most impactful charities to improve happiness and wellbeing where it’s needed most. We do this by conducting scientifically rigorous research into different cause areas, interventions, and charities. As world leading happiness experts, we strive to empower donors, researchers, and policymakers to maximise overall impact on global wellbeing. If you’re a donor looking to maximise your impact, we can help you. We will convert your donation into a Happiness Multiplier – enabling the most happiness your money can buy.
