Justice for Prosperity Foundation

The Justice for Prosperity Foundation works to expose actors that strip away our democratic freedoms and weaken our democratic processes. These actors project undemocratic ideals, disinformation, and hate into large (online) platforms, resulting in weakened democracies, polarization, and inequality as their actions lead to more domestic and international anti-rights impacts and conflicts. The organization conducts research, raises awareness, mobilizes everyday people and activists, and provides innovative and operational protection tools for vulnerable communities under siege such as human rights defenders, other CSOs, and (political) activists. By exposing and countering such actors, we preserve our democracies and allow the true representation of ideals of equality, and increase inclusive peacebuilding to flourish.
